# Math 140 v2 art direction

A curious sci-fi study adventure with expressive original cartoon silhouettes and warm painted anime environments. Rick and Morty and Studio Ghibli are visual references; the cast remains original KJ, Professor and B.O.T.

Use apricot, sage, deep teal, warm cream and brass. Ink is delicate rather than chunky black. Backgrounds have gouache texture, plants, soft daylight and useful empty space. Avoid flashing portals, harsh alarm sounds, crash gags and a humiliating WRONG stamp.

KJ wears an apricot hoodie and indigo trousers. Professor wears a sage vest, mustard bow tie and round glasses. B.O.T. has a teal egg silhouette, navy face and cream articulated arms. All three are welcoming and expressive. The PNG sprites are an initial art/acting reference, not finished jointed rigs.

Artwork: `public/art/v2/{classroom,stage,study,lab,kj,professor,bot}.png`. The four environments cover all six scenes. Characters use real alpha. Keep the blank boards free of generated lettering; exact mathematics and captions are code overlays.

Staging: student left, robot center, professor right; teaching board above them; captions inside a high-contrast rail at the bottom. Gentle entrance motion and camera drift are implemented. Replace the initial static facial poses with separate eyes, mouths and jointed gestures in the next character-animation pass.

Image provenance and the full built-in ImageGen prompts are in `image-prompts.json`; file checksums are in `asset-manifest.json`.
