# Math 140 v2 — Your Thinking, Your AI Sidekick

New adaptation for a friendlier alternate version. The original screenplay stays in production/screenplay.md.

## The Big Question

**KJ:** Could AI help us learn math, while we still do the thinking?

**BOT:** I can be your study sidekick! Where do we start?

**PROF:** With your questions. Learning is a team effort.

## The Five Rules

**KJ:** Our Math one-forty sidekick has five ground rules.

**PROF:** Let’s make them easy to remember.

**KJ:** One: build your own shapes.

**KJ:** Two: write your own proofs.

**BOT:** Could I show a practice example?

**PROF:** Yes! Then let the student try.

**KJ:** Three: ask for hints, not finished answers.

**PROF:** A good hint helps you choose the next step.

**KJ:** Four: explain your own reasoning.

**KJ:** Five: quizzes are your solo mission.

**BOT:** Study buddy now. Cheerleader at quiz time!

**PROF:** Exactly. You’re here to help us learn.

## Where Students Struggle

**KJ:** What makes a math problem feel confusing?

**PROF:** New words, tricky wording, and what the symbols mean.

**BOT:** Let’s turn those symbols into a picture.

**PROF:** That can help. Then explain the idea in your own words.

**KJ:** So understanding matters more than copying an answer?

**PROF:** Yes. Make the meaning yours.

## When AI Gets It Wrong

**BOT:** I got two plus two equals five. Wait…

**PROF:** Let’s check it together.

**KJ:** How do we catch an AI mistake?

**PROF:** Try a small example. Count two, then two more.

**PROF:** Four! Students and teachers both need to check AI’s work.

**BOT:** Thanks! Checking beats guessing.

## The Experiment

**KJ:** How could we test our study sidekick?

**PROF:** See whether it helps with vocabulary, reading problems, and understanding.

**KJ:** Would everyone need to use it?

**PROF:** We can offer a choice, with an approved practice activity.

**BOT:** A practice adventure! I’m ready.

**PROF:** And we’ll ask what helped, and what didn’t.

## The Final Verdict

**KJ:** Would you try a small pilot?

**PROF:** Yes. Let’s learn from it together.

**BOT:** Study-sidekick mode: ready!

**PROF:** With students steering the thinking.

**BOT:** I’ll bring the hints. You bring the ideas.

**KJ:** Think first. Ask for help. Check the work. That’s learning together.
