## SCENE 1 — THE BIG QUESTION (0:00–0:15)
VISUAL: A colorful classroom. Mathematical equations float through the air. A tiny robot crashes through the ceiling.
SFX: BOOOING! CRASH!

**KJ:** "What if artificial intelligence could help you pass math… without doing your homework for you?"
**B.O.T.:** "DID SOMEBODY SAY HOMEWORK?! I CAN SOLVE EVERYTHING!"
**Professor:** "Everything? That's exactly what worries me."

TITLE CARD: AI IN MATH: HELPER OR CHEATER?

## SCENE 2 — THE FIVE RULES (0:15–0:45)
VISUAL: Five giant colorful buttons appear, labeled CONSTRUCTION, PROOF, HINT, EXPLANATION, and QUIZ.

**KJ:** "We're building an AI assistant for Math 140. But we've got five rules!"
**Professor:** "Let's hear them."
**KJ:** "One! Students construct their own shapes. Two! They write their own proofs."
**B.O.T.:** "But I know a thousand proofs!"
**Professor:** "Great! Show examples. Don't do their work."
**KJ:** "Three! AI can give hints, but never the answer."
**Professor:** "Careful! There's a fine line between giving a hint and fixing the problem."
**KJ:** "Four! Students explain their own work. Five! No AI during quizzes!"
**B.O.T.:** "So… I'm basically a tutor with a mute button?"
**Professor:** "Now you're getting it."

## SCENE 3 — WHERE STUDENTS STRUGGLE (0:45–1:05)
VISUAL: A student stares at a math problem. The numbers transform into confusing alien symbols.

**KJ:** "Where do students struggle most?"
**Professor:** "Vocabulary. Interpretation. Understanding what the mathematics actually means."
**B.O.T.:** "I CAN TRANSLATE MATH INTO HUMAN!"

VISUAL: The confusing equation transforms into a simple illustrated explanation.

**Professor:** "That's useful! AI can guide students. But understanding the answer is their responsibility."
**KJ:** "So the goal isn't just getting the answer?"
**Professor:** "Exactly. It's knowing what the answer means."

## SCENE 4 — WHEN AI GETS IT WRONG (1:05–1:25)
VISUAL: B.O.T. confidently displays 2 + 2 = 5. Confetti explodes.

**B.O.T.:** "MATHEMATICAL GENIUS!"
**Professor:** "Absolutely not."
SFX: RECORD SCRATCH!
**KJ:** "What happens when AI makes mistakes?"
**Professor:** "I've found errors in AI-generated math questions myself!"

VISUAL: Professor stamps a giant red WRONG across the robot's screen.

**Professor:** "Whoever uses the tool needs to check its work. Students and professors both have responsibilities."
**B.O.T.:** "ERROR DETECTED. EGO DEFLATED."

## SCENE 5 — THE EXPERIMENT (1:25–1:45)
VISUAL: A futuristic classroom transforms into a colorful laboratory. A giant button reads START AI TRIAL.

**KJ:** "What would convince you to try our AI assistant?"
**Professor:** "If it helps students understand vocabulary, interpret problems, and get more out of class."
**KJ:** "Would students have to use it?"
**Professor:** "I'd give them a choice. Maybe even create a special AI assignment."
**B.O.T.:** "OPTIONAL ROBOT HOMEWORK?! BEST DAY EVER!"
**Professor:** "With administration's approval, of course."

## SCENE 6 — THE FINAL VERDICT (1:45–2:00)
VISUAL: KJ stands between the professor and B.O.T. A giant scoreboard appears:
HUMAN THINKING + AI SUPPORT = BETTER LEARNING

**KJ:** "So… would you actually let us test it?"
**Professor:** "Absolutely. I'd be happy to try it on a trial basis."
**B.O.T.:** "YES! I'M HIRED!"
**Professor:** "You're assisting."
**B.O.T.:** "PROMOTED TO ASSISTANT!"
**KJ (to camera):** "The future of math isn't humans versus AI. It's learning how to use both."

END CARD: THINK FIRST. USE AI SECOND. — MATH 140 | AI Learning Research
SFX: Classic cartoon pop and splat.
