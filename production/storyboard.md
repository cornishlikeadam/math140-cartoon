# Storyboard & Shot List
## Math 140 — AI vs. the Human Brain (120s / 3,600 frames @ 30 FPS)

---

### Scene 1: The Big Question (0:00–0:15 / Frames 0–450)
- **Setting:** Vibrant classroom with chalk doodles (`a² + b² = c²`, `π ≈ 3.14`), clock, and MATH 140 pennant.
- **Action:**
  - KJ opens the interview with a handheld microphone addressing the audience.
  - Floating equations drift across the air.
  - Frame 195: Audio whoosh; B.O.T. crashes through the ceiling with a jagged crack (`CRASH! BOOOING!`).
  - Screen shakes on impact. B.O.T. enthusiastically shouts: *"DID SOMEBODY SAY HOMEWORK?!"*
  - Professor raises skeptical eyebrow: *"Everything? That's exactly what worries me."*
  - Frame 345: Comic title card slams onto screen: **AI IN MATH: HELPER OR CHEATER?**

---

### Scene 2: The Five Rules (0:15–0:45 / Frames 450–1350)
- **Setting:** TV Game-show Stage with marquee lights, spotlight cones, and magenta sunburst.
- **HUD:** Five giant interactive buttons appear overhead: `1. CONSTRUCTION`, `2. PROOF`, `3. HINT`, `4. EXPLANATION`, `5. QUIZ`.
- **Action:**
  - KJ walks through the 5 rules at a brisk cartoon pace.
  - Manim video monitor displays Euclid construction and angle-sum proof.
  - Gag: When B.O.T. brags about knowing 1,000 proofs, a hazard-striped **HUMAN OVERSIGHT** censor bar slaps over its screen with a cartoon `BONK`.
  - Professor points with his wooden pointer, emphasizing boundary lines.

---

### Scene 3: Where Students Struggle (0:45–1:05 / Frames 1350–1950)
- **Setting:** Cozy warm study room / library with bookshelves and desk lamp.
- **Action:**
  - Professor explains where students struggle: *Vocabulary, interpretation, understanding.*
  - Floating alien math hieroglyphics appear.
  - B.O.T. announces: *"I CAN TRANSLATE MATH INTO HUMAN!"*
  - Manim video appears: animated rectangle dividing into a triangle, showing visual area representation (`Area = ½ × 6 × 4 = 12`).

---

### Scene 4: When AI Gets It Wrong (1:05–1:25 / Frames 1950–2550)
- **Setting:** Classroom with ceiling crack still visible.
- **Action:**
  - B.O.T. displays `2 + 2 = 5` with triumphant confetti cannon.
  - Professor dryly cuts in: *"Absolutely not."* (Record scratch SFX).
  - Professor stamps a giant red **WRONG!** across B.O.T.'s face screen.
  - Manim number line animation corrects the work with orange hops to `4`.
  - B.O.T.'s digital LED eyes droop: *"ERROR DETECTED. EGO DEFLATED."*

---

### Scene 5: The Experiment (1:25–1:45 / Frames 2550–3150)
- **Setting:** Futuristic laboratory with bubbling test tubes, oscilloscope graph, and tesla coil sparks.
- **Action:**
  - A pedestal in the center holds a giant red button: **START AI TRIAL**.
  - KJ asks what would convince the professor to run a trial.
  - Professor agrees to a trial with administrative approval.
  - B.O.T. celebrates: *"OPTIONAL ROBOT HOMEWORK?! BEST DAY EVER!"* (Tada fanfare).

---

### Scene 6: The Final Verdict (1:45–2:00 / Frames 3150–3600)
- **Setting:** Stage in victory blue lighting.
- **Action:**
  - Giant digital scoreboard lights up: **HUMAN THINKING + AI SUPPORT = BETTER LEARNING**.
  - Professor confirms trial basis; B.O.T. celebrates promotion to assistant.
  - KJ turns directly to the camera: *"The future of math isn't humans versus AI. It's learning how to use both."*
  - Frame 3420: Graphic End Card slams down:
    - **THINK FIRST. USE AI SECOND.**
    - **MATH 140 | AI Learning Research**
