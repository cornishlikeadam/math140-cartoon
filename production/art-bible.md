# Art Bible: Math 140 Cartoon Production

## 1. Visual Style & Aesthetics
- **Inspiration:** Early-2000s American Saturday morning television animation.
- **Outlines:** Thick, hand-drawn wobbly ink outlines (8px main outline, 5px thin interior).
- **Line Boil:** Simulated cel redrawing via SVG fractal turbulence displacement re-seeded every 3 frames.
- **Palette:** High-saturation primary and secondary tones on rich warm paper/slate backgrounds. Dark purple ink (`#1b1530`), never sterile pure black.

## 2. Character Designs

### KJ (Interviewer / Student)
- **Role:** Energetic, curious, college student host.
- **Silhouette:** Spiky dark hair, bright orange "140" hoodie, blue denim jeans, red-striped sneakers.
- **Props:** Handheld broadcast microphone with red "140" cube.
- **Acting:** Dynamic arm gestures, forward-leaning posture, direct eye contact with camera.

### The Professor (Mathematics Instructor)
- **Role:** Intelligent, approachable, mildly sarcastic mathematician.
- **Silhouette:** Tall, lanky, bald crown with silver side tufts, prominent friendly nose, round glasses.
- **Attire:** Purple argyle sweater vest, crisp white collared shirt, yellow & red bow tie, gray slacks.
- **Props:** Wooden pointing stick, stack of graded quizzes, giant red "WRONG!" ink stamp.

### B.O.T. (Overenthusiastic AI Assistant)
- **Role:** Hyperactive floating robot with an animated LED matrix face.
- **Silhouette:** Capsule teal chassis, articulated mechanical claws, top antenna with blinking alert bulb, rocket thruster flame exhaust.
- **Digital Screen:** Responsive LED face displaying mouth shapes, smug squint, question marks, and error alerts.
- **Running Gag:** A black-and-yellow hazard bar labeled **HUMAN OVERSIGHT** slaps over its screen whenever it attempts to give away solutions.

## 3. Typography & Sound
- **Titles & Badges:** *Luckiest Guy* (Google Fonts) — chunky, rounded comic book titling.
- **Impact SFX:** *Bangers* (Google Fonts) — classic action sound text.
- **Dialogue & UI:** *Fredoka* (Google Fonts) — friendly, readable geometric sans.
- **Soundtrack:** Original 144 BPM cartoon swing theme, synchronized to bar lines matching each scene.
