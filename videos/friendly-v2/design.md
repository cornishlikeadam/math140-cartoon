# Design truth

Concept: an inviting math workshop where curiosity is a shared adventure.

Palette: warm cream `#fff8e9`, sage/teal board `#173d38`, apricot `#ffd2a0`, light sage `#bceadd`, caption rail `#162b2c`, brass `#d7bc88`.

Use warm painted 16:9 environments for depth. KJ is the left foreground anchor, the professor the right anchor, B.O.T. the middle helper. A clear dark upper board is the learning focal point; a high-contrast bottom rail carries one line at a time. Headings use serif, captions use system sans; all fonts remain local generic families for this proof. Body captions are 33px at 1080p.

Motion: gentle staggered arrivals, bounded sine idle for only the floating robot and a slow background camera push. No flashing, violent shake or continuous scaling of text. The sprites are art references and initial blocking; facial animation requires a subsequent rig pass.
